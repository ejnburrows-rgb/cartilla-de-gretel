# One final recording manifest

[CSV](FINAL-RECORDING-MANIFEST.csv) is generated by scripts/audio/build-final-recording-manifest.py from the existing authored recording/vocabulary registries and canonical book transcriptions. Regenerate with Python3 from any directory. The generator verifies recovered files exist and records their SHA256.

244 deduplicated rows: 45 recovered recording files (40 instruction master cuts and five preserved Lesson3 cuts); 161 existing picture files carrying existing registry approval; 38 picture-registry gaps. The instruction mapping covers 109 regions. One file may have many printed uses. The 38 gaps match MISSING_PICTURE_RECORDINGS.csv exactly and are not 38 approved requests for new recordings.

Spanish wording is comparison text from existing authored sources. The preserved success cue has no verified exact transcript here and is deliberately blank. Registry labels are not silently promoted to spoken scripts. No filenames are treated as evidence of what a recording says. The original-master/transcript source policy still applies. Audio approval remains false until the owner actually listens and approves; this package does not activate recovered recordings or override historical provenance questions.
