# Gretel Tutor Demo Mode specification

This is the canonical product and acceptance specification for the Gretel Tutor in [draft PR #1073](https://github.com/ejnburrows-rgb/cartilla-de-gretel/pull/1073), branch `feat/gretel-tutor-l1-3`. It records Emilio’s torso-up camera decision of November 28, 2026. It supersedes conflicting framing, motion, scope and acceptance notes in earlier Tutor plans, renders and handoffs. The original Workbook and Teacher Flip Chart govern curriculum content, and the [Gretel linguistic standard](GRETEL-LINGUISTIC-STANDARD.md) governs Spanish dialogue. [Issue #877](https://github.com/ejnburrows-rgb/cartilla-de-gretel/issues/877) tracks implementation status and evidence.

## Camera and performance

- **Gretel is always torso-up, as in a real phone or video call.** This applies to every visible frame, including idle, speaking, listening, thinking, turning, writing, underlining or circling, erasing, returning, transitions, and reduced-motion alternatives. No full-body shot is shown. Apply this on desktop and phone, in call view, split screen, and movable, resizable picture-in-picture.
- Center Gretel within her own camera frame. She faces and addresses the child directly while talking or listening. Only move off-center for board work. Keep the torso-up crop during the turn, with enough room to see her shoulder, elbow, hand, tool and the mark. Return to the centered call pose before she addresses the child again. Keep the subject large and avoid empty bands that squeeze the classroom.
- Preserve the approved painted face, blue eyes, golden curls, red bow, orange striped blouse and blue embroidered pinafore. Use [the approved portrait](../../public/cartilla/images/gretel/tutor/reference/gretel-tutor-portrait-call.jpg). Preserve the deep-green board, vintage wooden frame, handwritten title, chalk, felt eraser and warm lighting.
- Use varied, subtle blinks, gaze, breathing, brows, head and hair motion. Time listening, thinking and smiles to the exchange. Use coherent upper-body poses during turns and review them in actual playback. Reject stiff puppet motion, broken-looking elbows and finger-writing.

## Demo entry and teaching exchange

1. In existing Demo Mode, offer **Aprender con Gretel** alongside the existing choices. Open the normal Tutor using the selected demo student and lesson. Use the existing picker if no lesson was selected. Leaving and returning must resume that demo student's place.
2. In the normal lesson, the child answers. Gretel acknowledges the response, turns to the board, holds visible chalk and writes the correct letter or syllable. The chalk tip meets the stroke. She underlines or circles the target, takes a visible felt eraser, and removes only marks where the felt touches the board.
3. Gretel turns back, returns to center, speaks using her authentic recording, and listens again. Rhubarb mouth shapes follow the actual media clock of the exact recording, including pauses and return to rest. Preserve replay, interruption and the typed response fallback.
4. Apply the exchange wherever the curriculum calls for it in all 24 lessons. The application selects the Spanish text, spelling, accents and target. A baked Lesson 3 “a” or “abeja” demonstration must never substitute for another target.
5. Preserve Workbook and Teacher Flip Chart behavior, offline speech recognition, dialogue selection, camera, transcript, split screen, movable and resizable picture-in-picture, saved progress, and teacher review. Keep children’s speech on-device. Fit long teaching words readably, including with the transcript open.

## Voice and source intake

Use owner-approved authentic recordings. Reconcile the owner’s recording inventory, the 45 recovered clips, the original voice masters, and any Antigravity delivery. A spreadsheet mismatch alone does not establish a missing take. The five isolated vowels and all eight Lesson 3 practice words passed playback at the November 28 checkpoint. The live [“¡Muy bien!”](recording/PRAISE.md) uses the verified original master excerpt. Keep private masters out of Git.

Return one deduplicated list of genuinely missing takes with exact Spanish and where the child hears each. If the book and approved dialogue leave wording undecided, include the exact proposed sentence. Emilio may send one continuous recording with pauses. The implementing agent cuts, names, aligns and integrates it using the [intake instructions](intake/README.md). Continue independent work during intake.

## Acceptance and delivery

- Build the expected ID list from current lesson files, Workbook and Flip Chart. For each page or sheet record its ID, lesson, expected content, speech or intentional silence, teaching action and actual result. Open every available ID through the app at desktop and phone sizes. Verify content, next/back and resume. Fix and revisit failed IDs, reconcile expected and observed IDs, and report unavailable originals and how the app handles them. Earlier checks accounted for 150 available IDs out of 152 expected. Recheck Workbook originals 86–87 against current sources before relying on that count.
- Personally watch each distinct teaching movement and layout, and listen to the actual recordings in normal playback. Check stable torso-up framing through all transitions, visible tool contact, selective erasing, and exact lip sync. Test correct and incorrect answers, retry, replay, interruption, offline use, reduced motion, camera/mic denial and saved progress. Distinguish automated results from firsthand observations. Follow the [repeatable Demo checks](DEMO-CHECKS.md).
- Push the work to the existing draft. Verify the same authorized preview the owner will open, starting from Demo Mode. Deliver one Demo Mode entry link, the GitHub revision, before-and-after views, page results and exceptions, and playable recordings with sound on desktop and phone. Label simulated phone footage. Begin both captures with the Demo selection and show a complete exchange and a different teaching target in a second lesson. Retain the repeatable checks and recording maintenance instructions in the repo.
- Update #877 at significant milestones with what works, pages passed out of pages expected, and what is still being fixed. Use “waiting only for Emilio’s audio or wording” only after all other acceptance checks pass and every remaining take or sentence is named. When audio arrives, integrate it and repeat the affected checks and Demo walkthrough.
- This draft work authorizes no paid work, replacement voice, Supabase changes, merge, or production deployment.
