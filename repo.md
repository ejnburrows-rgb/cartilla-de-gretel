# Locked Background Generation Method

> **Finish contract:** Read PROJECT_FINISH_DEFINITION.md before planning or declaring Cartilla work complete. It is the canonical definition of what must be true for the entire project to be finished. Compare current verified reality against it and close only real remaining gaps.


Applies whenever a new scenic background is explicitly approved for the **Workbook** or **Flip Chart**. It defines how to generate a background asset; it does **not** require every page or surface to render one.

## Surface-use rule
- **Student Workbook:** dense learner exercises use the owner-approved clean digital canvas. Do not use a complete scenic image as full-page wallpaper behind dense exercises. Existing scenic assets are preserved and may be used only in explicitly approved Workbook contexts where they do not compete with lesson content.
- **Teacher Flip Chart:** defaults to a clean paper surface **without** a scenic background-image layer; preserve source instructional foreground scenes and any historical scenic assets on disk. Generating a scenery file under this method never grants permission to mount it by default.
- This distinction changes background **usage**, not source structure, foreground art, or lesson content.

## Workflow
- Generate **one page at a time**. Never batch pages.
- Use the actual page topic, existing foreground illustration, intended setting, and required open area as inputs.
- Generate **background only**.
- Do not recreate, replace, modify, redraw, recolor, or invent the original foreground characters, objects, text, or lesson content.
- The background must be a complete living scene, not a border, frame, or plain gradient.
- Keep the reserved area visually clear so the original illustration and text can be composited on top.

## Visual Direction
Classic Latin American children's cartilla, circa 2000s, combined with **pintura country / tole one-stroke folk painting**.

Use:
- opaque craft acrylic / acrylic-gouache appearance;
- separate clean painted silhouettes;
- warm sienna-brown outlines, never black;
- bold, saturated flat colors;
- minimal interior shading;
- one-stroke leaves, petals, and decorative botanical details;
- small paint-marker / stylus-dot accents;
- subtle uniform shadows for layered cut-out depth;
- clean printed-schoolbook finish.

The result must feel:
- vibrant;
- cheerful;
- nostalgic;
- richly scenic;
- child-friendly;
- stylized like the original Cartilla art;
- lively without becoming visually busy.

## Scenic Construction
Build an environment appropriate to the page: garden, pond, meadow, countryside, patio, bedroom, classroom, path, trees, flowers, hills, clouds, or other relevant scenery.

Supporting scenery should frame and lead toward the lesson content without competing with it.

## Palette
Use a vivid educational palette:
- cadmium red;
- cadmium orange;
- chrome yellow;
- magenta;
- emerald green;
- yellow-green;
- turquoise;
- cobalt blue;
- warm cream;
- sienna brown.

## Avoid
No watercolor or transparent washes, realism, photorealism, painterly blending, oil/impasto texture, visible paper or canvas grain, black outlines, muted/pastel palettes, anime, Pixar, CGI/3D, vector-flat app art, cinematic lighting, grunge, unrelated subjects, decorative border-only layouts, or plain gradient-only backgrounds.

## Locked Rule
This is the approved default method whenever Workbook or Flip Chart background-image generation is explicitly authorized. It is not a requirement to generate or render a background on every page. The surface-use rules above govern both Student Workbook and teacher Flip Chart: a generated background may be displayed only in the exact context explicitly approved by the owner, never as default Flip Chart wallpaper.


## Relationship to foreground color transfer

This file governs **background generation only**. Its instruction not to recolor foreground art means a background-generation task must never alter foreground pixels. The separate owner-approved source-preserving foreground color-transfer workflow is governed by `ASSET_FIDELITY_POLICY.md` and is not performed as part of background generation.
