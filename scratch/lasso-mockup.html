What's deliberately different from a generic kids' app

The verb matches the gesture

"Circular" (circle) doesn't describe a tap on glass. "Enlazar" (lasso/rope) is something Gretel visibly does to the word — the instruction and the motion are the same idea.

Feedback is her, not an icon

Duolingo's Duo is the reference point here: the mascot reacts in-line to every answer using real character states, not a floating badge. Your video clips replace the ✓/✕ entirely.

Motion has one job at a time

The rope throw is the only large motion on screen — everything else (ambient float, hover) stays subtle so the catch reads as the moment, not noise.
