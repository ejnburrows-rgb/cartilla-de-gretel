# Cartilla Artwork Optimization Standard — SUPERSEDED FOR BOOK PAGES

The previous optimization/remaster instructions in this file are retired for the teacher Flip Chart / Flipbook and Student Workbook.

Use `BOOK_FIDELITY_RULE.md` and `AGENTS.md`.

## Current rule

- Book-page images are already fixed/cropped and are locked.
- Do not recolor, remaster, regenerate, redraw, recrop, retouch, restyle, replace, or transform book-page artwork.
- Do not rebuild a book page from standalone “modernized” objects.
- Use the authoritative physical-book PDFs to determine placement, scale, order, geometry, and page composition.
- The Flip Chart must remain visually faithful to the Flip Chart.
- The Workbook must remain visually faithful to the Workbook.

Any prior Gretel 2.0 / modernized-finish guidance may apply only to separately authorized non-book app artwork. It is not authorization to modify the pages or images of either book.
